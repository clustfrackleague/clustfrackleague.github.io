League overview

Welcome to the home of Clusterfrack 2 — the league's public record for members and anyone else who wants to follow along. Everything about how the league runs, who plays in it, and what its history holds is on this page: the format and rules up top, then all-time records, honours, season-by-season standings, and a full tab for every franchise below.

Playoffs

Top 4 in each division make the playoffs — 8 of the league's 10 teams qualify for the championship bracket.

2026 only — crossover rule: a 5th-place team with a better record than the other division's 4th-place team takes that spot.

Playoff tiebreaker

When teams are tied, seeding is decided in this order:

  • 1. Points for — most points scored comes first
  • 2. Head-to-head record — result between the tied teams

League honours

Three honours are on the line every season:

  • The Drew McLean Trophy — league champion
  • The Payton — regular season winner
  • The Rudy — Toilet Bowl, last place

Every past winner is listed on the Honours Board below.

            Championship probability — how it works

            Each franchise's number is simply titles ÷ playoff appearances — how often they've converted a playoff trip into the Drew McLean Trophy. Across the league, 32 playoff trips produced 4 champions, a 12.5% base rate (exactly what you'd expect with 8 teams in the bracket). Graeme's 50% (2 titles in 4 trips) is four times the base rate; Tye and Dan (Pass the Dutch) sit at 33.3% (1 in 3). Everyone else is still looking for their first conversion — Nathan has reached two finals without winning one, the league's hard-luck line.
